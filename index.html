👤 About Background & Philosophy

Background

I'm a product design engineer with hands-on experience in development, testing, and root cause analysis. My work lives at the intersection of how things are made, why they fail, and how teams can move smarter.

Beyond the bench and the CAD model, I'm drawn to strategy — asking not just how to build, but why it matters and whether it makes financial sense.

I treat thinking as a craft. Mental models, first principles, and cross-disciplinary learning aren't extras — they're the method.
